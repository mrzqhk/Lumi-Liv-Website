MACHINE 02

Fiber Laser
Engraving Machine

Engineered for metals and hard surfaces — this machine burns permanent, razor-sharp marks without touching the material.

30W Power
110x110mm Field
+-0.03mm Accuracy

Unlike CO₂ lasers, fiber lasers mark without heat distortion — ideal for jewellery, trophies, stainless steel tags, and permanent branding.

What we can make with it

Metal Trophy & Award Engraving

Names, dates, logos, and citations laser-etched permanently on stainless steel, aluminium, and brass trophies, plaques, and medals.
